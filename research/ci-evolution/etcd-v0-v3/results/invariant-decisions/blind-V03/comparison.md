# Judgment versus independent update-local generation

The frozen V03 judgment proposed a local crossing-effect rule and flagged deferred-application progress as unresolved (U4/U9). It did not supply a usable progress predicate. It did not confidently claim progress needed no change.

The separate generation call received old/new source, the update, and a neutral state vocabulary, but no old invariants, first judgment, gold labels, or model-development reports. Candidate C03 explicitly preserves an automatic-exit obligation across leadership changes and deferred ApplyConfChange, and excludes mutable pending-index crossing from the liveness antecedent. It supplies a new-leader witness (applied=old last=barrier; leader no-op advances past it).

This is a concrete gain in candidate coverage in one developmental experiment, not a general accuracy estimate. Candidate truth, environment assumptions, reachability and source consequence remain to be tested. Its cost was higher than the direct judgment; there is no evidence here for regenerating every property on every update.
