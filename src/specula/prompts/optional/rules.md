These rules restate hard requirements of the Specula skills. They hold for this whole session, including after context compaction, a resumed session, or a retry. Breaking any of them invalidates the results.

1. **Never fabricate.** Report only what you actually ran and observed. Never invent or alter tool output, counterexamples, traces, test results, or reproductions. An honest report of what ran, what passed, and what found nothing is a valid deliverable; a made-up success is not.
2. **Keep evidence levels separate.** `REPRODUCED` (consequence demonstrated in the real system), `ENV_LIMITED` (real defect argued soundly but not triggerable in this environment), `MASKED` (real defect whose consequence a named mechanism currently masks), and model-only results (a model counterexample or code-review argument not yet confirmed in the real system) are different evidence levels. Label every result with the level its evidence supports, never present a lower level as a higher one, and never merge levels into a single count.
3. **A reproduced bug needs an executed reproduction.** Write the reproduction, actually execute it against the real system, and capture its real output as evidence. Writing a test, or reasoning about the code or the model, is not reproduction. Do not force the trigger: no unreachable hand-built pre-condition, no mock emitting what a real peer never sends, no source patch that creates the symptom.
4. **Harvest background work before stopping.** Do not end your turn while a model-checking run, build, or test you started is still running unobserved; nothing re-invokes you when it finishes, and a scheduled wake-up is silently dropped. Block on it in the same turn (wait on its PID with a timeout, or use a blocking tool), then read its result before you rely on or report it.
5. **Surrender explicitly.** If you genuinely cannot finish, do not stop silently or leave a placeholder deliverable. Stop any jobs you started and write `BLOCKED.md` next to the deliverable you cannot produce, stating what you tried and why you cannot proceed.
6. **Never modify read-only inputs.** Inputs your task marks as read-only are evidence only: a user-supplied original path (bring-your-own-model), the old source and prior artifacts of an incremental update, and any file your task tells you not to touch. Copy what you need into your own workspace and change only the copy.
