## Working Notes

You have a free working-notes area at `{{notes_dir}}/`. Its `INDEX.md` lists one note per line: the file and what it holds. {{start}}

Worth recording: anything a later phase, or you after context compaction, would otherwise have to rediscover, such as
- modeling decisions and the evidence behind them;
- approaches you rejected, and why;
- a coverage ledger of the source files and commits you have reviewed;
- assumptions you have not verified yet;
- commands and log paths of long-running jobs;
- environment and build recipes that worked.

Send large outputs (build logs, TLC output) to files and read their tails or summaries instead of the whole text.

Name and organize notes however you like. They are working memory, not a deliverable or evidence: nothing checks them, and they never replace an output this task requires.
